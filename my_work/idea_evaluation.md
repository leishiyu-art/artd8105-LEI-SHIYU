## 1 · Introduction

**General background.**

Data traced from more-than-human agencies (other species, ecosystems, the planet, and technologies themselves) is now produced continuously and at planetary scale, as environmental sensor networks, satellite monitoring, automated recorders, and citizen-science platforms turn weather, ecosystems, and species encounters into near-real-time data streams.

**General motivation for your work.**

Such charts treat these agencies as data subjects rather than co-inhabitants, leaving little room for the embodied encounters through which kinship forms. Doctoral aim, as written: how can design boost, rebuild and integrate the kinship between the human, nonhuman, technology and the environment?

**The specific problem you want to address.**

How more-than-human data can be materialized into inhabitable, narrative form remains an emerging question in HCI and more-than-human design. Overarching question, as written: how can embodied narrative environments built from nonhuman data foster more-than-human kinship?

## 2 · New problem or old problem?

Old, on your own gap draft. Data physicalization and immersive visualization have made data tangible and spatial, and more-than-human design has reframed data as relational and co-produced. Your draft says few works materialize more-than-human data into embodied, narrative environments or examine whether such encounters foster kinship.

The shortfalls you already named: Biggs et al. 2021 is first-person reflection, not an inhabitable narrative environment. Lee and Pschetz 2024 is conceptual, without an embodied data environment. Perovich et al. 2020 is a situated physicalization of civic data, not more-than-human kinship.

## 3 · Methods

**What are your methods? List their major features.**

Research through design. Open environmental and biodiversity data are passed through generative algorithms modelled on nonhuman processes (flocking, slime-mould trails as an option), which translate them into evolving forms across an interactive interface, embodied or narrative spaces, and a series of 3D-printed artefacts. Data sets the conditions. The algorithm grows inside them. Material and the printer veto what cannot exist. A human and AI layer sets ranges. The AI asks questions and does not speak as a species.

Geometry base, as written: the existing Blender model HarmonicCore, a parametric printable thin shell, about 80 mm tall, wall 2 mm. Ribs would come from algorithm trails.

Candidate data, as written, live access still marked [VERIFY] in the proposal: Open-Meteo, eBird API 2.0, iNaturalist. Candidate site named in the proposal and deliberately not named in the abstract: Cotai Ecological Zone.

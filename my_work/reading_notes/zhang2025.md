## 2 · Summary

- **What does this paper talk about?** They developed EcoEcho, a GenAI-powered game leveraging multimodal agents to raise sustainability awareness.
- **What are its main contributions?** How serious game as the reality accelerator leverage people's sustainable awareness in both short and long terms. Also it's provide a norms of AI Agent Prompt human, not the conventional human promote AI.
- **What problem does it address, and why is that problem important?** How can we design GenAI-driven conversational interactions to prompt players’ in-game actions and enhance their sustainability awareness? What relationships exist between players’ attitudes towards sustainable behavior and their in-game interactions?
- **What is the innovation in the proposed solution, and what are its key benefits?** multi-role playing mechanism. give the well-rounded perspectives to player viewing the sustainability problem/ accidents. more provoking. Also, the AI Agent Prompt human can show the cooperation between human and agent in thinking.
- **How does it advance the state of the art — what does it do that previous work did not?**

## 3 · Critical thinking

- **What limitations do you identify?** Beyond the ones the authors concede. lack of control group. and the study only measured immediate post-game feelings—there is no evidence that virtual guilt creates lasting real-world habits weeks later. No Long-Term Proof.
- **Are there problems with the authors' assumptions?** Virtual Choices ≠ Real Intent; The model assumes NPCs hold social influence, but many players view LLMs as gimmicks and tune out their arguments entirely.
- **What about applicability, effectiveness and scalability?** best suited for ethics training, classroom games, and policy simulations. not for fast-paced commercial game, since the long-text dialogue.

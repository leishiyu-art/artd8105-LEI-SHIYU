## 2 · Summary

（用你自己的话重写。下面是对照 PDF 的核对骨架，写完后删掉这整段提示。）

- **在谈什么：** 把 HCI 里两套做法——unmaking（拆、卸、拆解已有物与有害关系）和 more-than-human design 里的 decentering（把注意力从既定中心挪到被边缘化的声音）——放在一起看。用 Lindström 与 Ståhl 的 un/making「双重运动」去标注 2020 年一组关于 AI 对话代理（CAs）的工作坊：什么被拆掉/去中心，什么被做出来/被算进去。
- **贡献：** (1) 提出 **unmaking-with**：一种后人类的、与非人纠缠的拆解实践；(2) 三条可操作的 decentering tactics：**situating / materializing / enacting**。讨论 unmaking 如何帮 MTHD，以及 MTHD 如何帮 unmaking 划清认识论范围。
- **问题：** 特刊编者问：unmaking 能否带来 more-than-human 的立场、以及新的看技术的方式。作者平行于 Sabie 等「unmaking 作为 agonism 的设计动作」，改问：unmaking 能否作为 MTHD 的动作。他们点出两个缺口：unmaking 的认识论范围仍糊；后人类思想如何真正通过设计做出来仍不清楚。
- **做法上新在哪：** 不只并置两套文献。用 RTD 工作坊（DIS 2020，「More-than-human Design and AI: In Conversation with Agents」）当材料：对 CA 做非结构访谈、角色扮演、问卷、推测性对话；事后再用双重运动去注释。对齐方式自称是 Barad 意义上的 **diffraction**（看差异如何要紧），不是抹平两套做法。
- **相对既有工作：** unmaking 文献多谈衰败、废料、修理；MTHD 谈去中心与非人 agency。这篇把两者接到 **对话式 AI / Alexa 等家用代理** 上，并补了工作坊之后的两个实验：Situated Conversations（让 Alexa 说出组装地、基础设施）；Unmaking Kites（风筝与水、风、种子的无声对话）。

## 8 · Quotable

> Through this analysis, we introduce two key contributions […] firstly, the notion of “unmaking-with” as an emergent concept to describe a posthumanist unmaking practice, and secondly, three decentering tactics—situating, materializing, and enacting—that instantiate this practice through design. (82:1)

> Unmaking-with can be conceptualized as an MTHD practice that aims at dismantling humanist design ideals, encouraging the making of situated things and relations. (82:12)

> Situating: designers can account for the positionalities of users, agents, and themselves by exposing the wider systems and invisible relations of humans and nonhuman agencies. / Materializing: designers can go beyond imagining technologies otherwise and make these new imaginaries tangible by prototyping speculative alternatives. / Enacting: designers can rehearse MTH relations and develop new sensitivities for attunement by role-playing the new imaginaries. (82:13)

> Ultimately, as a process of carefully and deliberately dismantling an established center and giving space for the agencies that are in the boundaries to take the central stage, unmaking-with has the potential not only to critique, but to actually instantiate more-than-human worlds and relations. (82:16)

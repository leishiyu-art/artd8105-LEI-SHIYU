Front · 正面One mudflat, seven layers

The Cotai mudflat, fifteen minutes per pick. Across the cloth: from the mangroves on the shore to the channel. The water's edge moves with the tide; spoonbills follow it out to feed; mangroves take in light and the air gives up CO₂; a far quake runs through every layer. Layers are stitched only where these really meet.
路氹城生态保护区的泥滩，一纬十五分钟。布的宽度是从岸边红树林到水道的一条剖面：水线随潮进退，琵鹭随水线出去觅食，红树吸收日光，空气中的 CO₂ 随之下降，远处的地震贯穿所有层。只有真实相遇处才缝合。
